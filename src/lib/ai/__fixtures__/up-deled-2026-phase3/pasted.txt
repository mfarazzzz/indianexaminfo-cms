# 1. Purpose of the notice

### Title
**डीएलएड (बीटीसी) प्रशिक्षण 2026 प्रशिक्षण संस्थान का विकल्प भरने तथा आवंटित संस्थान में अभिलेखीय जाँच / प्रवेश की कार्यवाही पूर्ण किये जाने हेतु अंतिम अवसर**

In simple terms:

> **Final opportunity to fill the choice of D.El.Ed. (BTC) training institution and complete document verification/admission formalities at the allotted institution.**

The notice refers to the earlier advertisement dated **07.08.2026**, issued regarding the D.El.Ed. Training 2026 admission process.

Candidates who had applied online were being given the opportunity to submit training-institution choices in stages along with the merit process.

The notice states that the admission process at the allotted training institution following the earlier phase had been completed on **25.09.2026**.

After considering representations received from some candidates and private institutions, the authority has decided to provide **one final opportunity** in **Phase-3** for eligible candidates to fill their institution choice according to merit and subsequently complete the admission process.
