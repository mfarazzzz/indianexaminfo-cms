  1. Open the official UPSSSC website.
  2. Open the “Download Admit Card” link for PET 2026.
  3. Sign in with your registration number and date of birth.
  4. Enter your gender and the verification code, then submit.
  5. Download the PDF and print it.
  6. On exam day carry: the printout, a valid photo ID, and photographs.
